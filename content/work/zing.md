Zing is HSBC's international money app for travellers and people living between
countries — sending, spending and holding multiple currencies without the usual
friction. I joined as a **Senior Frontend Software Engineer** in July 2023,
based in Haarlem, and have been on the product since.

## What I built

I pioneered the architecture and full-scale launch of the web app: the dashboard
where customers in the UK manage their core banking operations and see their
money visualised clearly. That meant owning the frontend from first technical
design through to deployment and post-launch optimisation, rather than picking
up someone else's foundation.

Working end-to-end across the SDLC gave me room to keep tuning after release.
A/B testing and sustained performance work improved Core Web Vitals by **30%**,
and the app holds an accessibility score of **93**.

## Working with the team

I mentored three junior developers, establishing the coding standards and
technical strategy the team works to. Code quality improved by **20%** and we
hold **86%** test coverage — numbers that came from making the standard
explicit and reviewable rather than tribal.

Alongside the customer-facing app, I collaborated with cross-functional teams on
maintaining and scaling the primary design system used across our back-office
applications.

## Stack

**Product:** Next.js (React 19), TypeScript, SCSS, Storybook, a headless CMS,
Argo CI/CD on AWS infrastructure, New Relic and Mixpanel for observability and
analytics, Checkmarx and WIZ for security.

**Back office:** micro-frontends in Angular 15 talking to Java (Spring Boot v3)
microservices.
