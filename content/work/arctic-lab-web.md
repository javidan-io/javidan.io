[Arctic Lab](https://arcticlab.io) is an online education platform for tech
courses, events and mentorship — cybersecurity, data analytics, business
analysis, AI and design, taught by people working at companies like AWS, Tesla
and Wise. I have led it as **Lead Full-Stack Engineer and Engineering Mentor**
since February 2025, part time and voluntary.

## Owning the product

I own the web app: running sprint planning, defining the product roadmap and
prioritising the features that move the platform forward. That extends to the
interface itself — designing the UI against product requirements, then closing
the loop by collecting user feedback and acting on it.

I engineered the full stack, client and server, including the integrations the
business runs on: **Stripe** for payments and **Cal.com** for scheduling.
Sustained work on SEO, performance and accessibility increased site visits by
**30%**.

## Mentoring

Alongside the engineering, I voluntarily guide five junior developers and interns
from the community, giving them technical direction and hands-on coding support
to accelerate their careers.

## Stack

**Frontend:** Next.js and TypeScript on the App Router, React Query, Zustand,
Tailwind CSS, with Jest and Playwright for testing.

**Backend:** Node.js, TypeScript and Express, Zod for validation, Pino for
logging, Prisma ORM over PostgreSQL, deployed on Heroku with Datadog for
observability.
