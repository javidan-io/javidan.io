At Paragraf in Tallinn I worked as a **Full-Stack Software Engineer** from June
2018 to June 2022 — four years spanning the core web app and the internal IT
infrastructure that kept it running.

I contributed across the whole lifecycle: writing user stories, developing,
testing, deploying and supporting what shipped. I also built the **CI/CD
pipeline** that automated builds, tests and deployments, which is where the
compounding return was — every release after it cost the team less.

## Stack

React with SCSS, React Router, Hooks (from v16.8), Redux and Axios on the
frontend; Node.js and Express on the server, running on Google Firebase
infrastructure.
